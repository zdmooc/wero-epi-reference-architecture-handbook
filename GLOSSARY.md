# Glossary — Seed

> Definitions will be expanded and formally sourced before publication.

## Acceptor
Role on the merchant-receiving side of a payment flow. Exact responsibilities depend on the payment model and integration.

## CSM
Clearing and Settlement Mechanism. Generic term that must not be used as a synonym for every rail or every settlement infrastructure.

## Clearing
Processes that route, validate, match, calculate or establish obligations before or around settlement, depending on the system design.

## Consumer PSP
Payment service provider serving the payer/consumer side in the reference model.

## DCA
Dedicated Cash Account. Meaning and use must always be tied to the specific TARGET/TIPS context being described.

## EPI
European Payments Initiative.

## Finality
Point at which a transfer or settlement becomes final according to the applicable system and legal framework.

## Idempotency
Property allowing the same logical request to be retried without creating a second unintended financial effect.

## ISO 20022
Financial messaging standard and modelling framework. It is not by itself a clearing or settlement rail.

## Liquidity
Funds or settlement capacity available to meet payment obligations.

## Payment Hub
Banking platform or integration layer that orchestrates payment processing across channels, schemes, controls and rails.

## Rail
Informal architecture term for the infrastructure/path used to move or settle payments. The book always names the exact scheme/CSM/settlement system rather than relying only on this word.

## Reconciliation
Process used to compare local records with authoritative or external records and resolve differences.

## RT1
Instant-payment system operated by EBA CLEARING. Exact settlement and funding mechanics are documented in the dedicated chapter from official sources.

## SCA
Strong Customer Authentication.

## Scheme
Set of business and technical rules governing participation and payment processing.

## SCT Inst
SEPA Instant Credit Transfer scheme.

## Settlement
Discharge of financial obligations through transfer of the settlement asset according to the applicable infrastructure.

## TIPS
TARGET Instant Payment Settlement.

## UNKNOWN
Reference state used when a potentially effective financial operation has no reliable final result locally. UNKNOWN is not equivalent to FAILED.

## VoP
Verification of Payee.

## Wero
European payment solution operated within the EPI ecosystem. Detailed public capabilities are versioned in the dedicated chapters.
