## Important meaning of COMPLETE

`COMPLETE` means that the architecture domain has a coherent first-edition chapter, source/governance model and cross-domain position in the manuscript.

It does **not** mean that every future annual update is complete forever.

The book remains a living reference.

## Release state

`v1.0.0-rc1` is the appropriate editorial state after I16.

A final physical-print release still requires:
- generated PDF proof;
- visual inspection of every figure/table;
- physical proof copy if print distribution is chosen;
- ISBN/distribution decisions where applicable.

Those are publishing operations, not missing architecture iterations.
