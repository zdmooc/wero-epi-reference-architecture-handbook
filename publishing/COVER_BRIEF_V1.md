# Cover Brief — V1.0

**Status:** DESIGN BRIEF READY / ARTWORK NOT FINAL

## Product

**Title**  
Wero & EPI — Architecture de Référence des Paiements Européens

**Subtitle**  
Paiements instantanés, ISO 20022, SCT Inst, TIPS, RT1, réseaux, cloud, sécurité, résilience et réglementation européenne

**Author**  
Djamal Zidane

## Positioning

Professional architecture handbook for:
- solution / enterprise / payment architects;
- banking and PSP technology teams;
- OpenShift/Kubernetes/platform architects;
- security/resilience/SRE practitioners;
- consultants working on European instant payments.

The cover must communicate:
- European payments;
- architecture depth;
- professional reference;
- resilience / infrastructure / rails;
- technical seriousness without looking like vendor marketing.

## Visual direction

Preferred:
- clean professional grid;
- strong typographic title;
- abstract architecture/network/rail motif;
- European-payment context without copying EPI/Wero brand assets;
- restrained palette derived from Visual Design System V1;
- readable at thumbnail size;
- no screenshot collage;
- no bank/client logos;
- no suggestion of official EPI/Wero sponsorship.

## Front cover hierarchy

1. title;
2. subtitle;
3. author;
4. optional small edition marker: `V1.0 — 2026`.

Do not put:
- internal project names;
- client brands;
- unverified marketing claims;
- certification/compliance claims.
