## Verified public basis

Official Wero/EPI materials identify **EPI Company SE** as the provider of the Wero Solution.

Official Wero terms define the “EPI Mark” broadly as trade names, names, words, logos, trademarks, designs, symbols and other distinctive signs owned or controlled by EPI.

Official Wero campaign rules also identify **EPI Company SE as owner of the WERO trademark**.

## Cover rule

Until an explicit licence is obtained:

- use the words “Wero” and “EPI” typographically as part of the book title;
- do not use official Wero/EPI logos or copy their visual identity;
- do not use bank/client logos;
- do not create badges or seals that suggest certification, approval or partnership.
