# Trademark & Independence Note — V1.0

**Date checked:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** EDITORIAL_REFERENCE / MANUSCRIPT_WORDING_IMPLEMENTED  
**Purpose:** publishing provenance; not legal advice

## V1.0 implemented approach

The digital V1.0 uses deliberately conservative wording in:

`book/00-front-matter/01-publication-note.md`

The rendered manuscript:
- identifies itself as an independent publication;
- does not claim sponsorship, endorsement or publication by EPI/Wero, ECB, EPC, EBA CLEARING or a bank;
- does not use official Wero/EPI logos as the book identity;
- uses names of organisations, schemes, services and products for identification and architecture analysis;
- states that associated marks and rights belong to their respective holders.

This approach avoids making the publication depend on a stronger trademark-status sentence.

## Public-source basis

Official Wero/EPI public materials checked on 2026-09-29 identify EPI Company SE as provider of the Wero solution and contain trademark/brand-rights provisions.

Those sources remain useful editorial references, but the book's V1.0 front matter intentionally uses the more general ownership/identification wording above.

## Cover rule

Unless an explicit licence exists:
- use “Wero” and “EPI” typographically as identifiers;
- do not use official logos or copy the Wero/EPI visual identity;
- do not use bank/client logos;
- do not create badges or seals suggesting certification, approval or partnership.

## Commercial / retail gate

No external legal review is claimed.

Before a commercial retail/print edition, an appropriate publication/legal review remains recommended if the cover, imprint, trademark wording, ISBN/barcode or sales claims materially change.
